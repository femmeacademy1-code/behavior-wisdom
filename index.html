Core principles

Observe first

Behavior is information. Pause before judging and notice what is actually happening.

Look for the need

Every action serves a need — safety, belonging, respect, or control. Find it, and the action makes sense.

Change the environment

Small shifts in context shape habits more reliably than willpower alone.

Lead by example

People mirror what they see. Consistent conduct teaches more than any speech.
